About us

Focused on the
DC Policy Ecosystem

Our mission is simple: simplify work on the Hill, strengthen advocacy, and unify both in one shared workflow platform. We’ve spent countless hours talking with congressional staff and advocates about their biggest pain points, and we’ve designed software that actually solves them.

Before CRMs, legislative offices sorted through buckets of physical mail. Today, much of the policy world still runs on decades-old tools—email chains, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems that are only marginally better than that bucket at your feet.

MIO changes that. We’re giving Hill staff tools that make work faster, smarter, and easier; giving advocates better ways to engage and collaborate; and creating a shared platform that connects everyone involved in moving policy forward. We’re committed to building even more solutions that improve efficiency, collaboration, and decision-making across the policy ecosystem for the long haul.

MIO Vote

The situation on the floor is more dynamic than ever but staff are still stuck communicating with their bosses via email and text

MIO brings together staff voting recommendations, live House floor information—including vote totals and the C-SPAN feed—and the latest leadership and whip guidance. Staff can collaborate in real time, adding rationale and supporting materials.

A simple mobile app gives your boss an instantly updated view of each vote, the office’s position, and the latest guidance, with the ability to click into any bill for more detail.

MIO alerts staff if the boss votes differently from the recommendation and stores every recommendation, rationale, and final vote in a searchable record.

Keep Track of All the Office Contacts

Someone requests a meeting and you don’t recognize the name. Or you run into someone at an event and want to understand their history with the office. MIO gives you one place to look them up in seconds and immediately see who they are and how they have engaged with your team.

Every relationship lives in one shared record. See every interaction a contact has had with the office—from meetings and communications to supporting a letter, joining a coalition, or attending an event—so staff always have the context they need before the next conversation.

Automatically Build a Searchable Record!

When a key staffer with all the institutional knowledge (and their unique filing system) leaves, it can take weeks to bring a new hire up to speed.

MIO makes that transition seamless. Every vote you’ve taken, letter you’ve signed, caucus you’ve joined, and bill you’ve cosponsored is automatically stored in a fully searchable database—so institutional memory never disappears when staffers move on.


Your boss comes to you and says “I have a meeting with John Doe in 30 minutes, what do I need to know?”

With MIO’s directory of all interactions with advocates, in seconds you can search that person’s name and know every interaction your office has had with them and what they care about.

Collaborate Seamlessly

Complex legislative projects shouldn’t be managed through endless email chains, scattered spreadsheets, and outdated attachments. MIO gives offices and outside stakeholders a shared, purpose-built workspace for collaborating on letters, bills, caucuses, press events, markups, and other coordinated efforts.

Participants can work from the latest documents and information, track meetings and communications, coordinate signers and cosponsors, and stay aligned as the project develops. Everyone sees the most current version, creating one clear place to manage the people, materials, and decisions behind the effort.

Keep the Office in Sync

Manage leave in one place. Staff can request time off, supervisors can review and approve it, and the office can track each employee’s available and used leave. Once approved, leave is automatically added to the appropriate shared calendar.

Coordinate schedules across the office. Create as many shared calendars as you need that update in real time, giving staff a clear view of meetings, events, deadlines, remote-work schedules, and who is in or out of the office.

MIO Advocate

Manage your advocacy work in one place. Organize contacts, coordinate fly-ins, track meetings and follow-ups, maintain relationship histories, and complete reporting requirements more efficiently.

Reach congressional staff inside their actual workflows. Communicate, schedule meetings, and share information through the same platform offices use to manage legislation and make decisions—moving important advocacy communications out of crowded inboxes and directly into the work itself.

Submit requests quickly and consistently. Send appropriations requests, meeting requests, and other submissions directly to participating offices without searching for separate forms or repeating the same administrative work.

By connecting your advocacy efforts directly with congressional workflows, MIO Advocate saves time, strengthens relationships, and helps turn your work into measurable wins.

Know Your History with Every Hill Office

You have an important meeting tomorrow. In seconds, see who on your team knows the office, which staffers they have worked with, what was discussed, and every way your organization has engaged with the member on and off the Hill. Walk in with the full history, even as staff change.

Keep contacts, meetings, event conversations, requests, and follow-ups in one shared record, so colleagues can coordinate outreach and avoid overwhelming an office with overlapping asks. Share interactions with your team when useful or keep them private when needed, while giving everyone the context to build stronger relationships and advocate more effectively.

Automatically Build a Searchable Record of Your Advocacy

You bring a new associate onto a client team. Instead of spending days piecing together the history, they can instantly see every meeting, Hill interaction, letter, bill, request, and result.

MIO Advocate preserves your team’s institutional knowledge. Search by client, issue, member, office, or date to see what your team has done, who was involved, what worked, and what did not. As team members change, the history stays with your organization, so new colleagues can get up to speed quickly.
